# **Forge Growth Brand Guide**

---

## **Typography System**

### **Primary Typeface: Oswald**

* Use for: Headlines, CTAs, logos, titles

* Weights: SemiBold / Bold for headers, Regular for nav/UI

* Visual Tone: Strong, modern, geometric

* Google Fonts: [Oswald](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald)

  ### **Secondary Typeface: `Inter`**

* Use for: Body text, report content, long-form paragraphs

* Visual Tone: Clean, readable, minimalist

* Google Fonts: [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)

  ### **System Font Backup**

* Primary: `Arial Black` or `Impact`

* Secondary: `Verdana` or `Segoe UI`

  ---

  ## **Color Palette**

| Color | Role | Hex |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Burnt Orange | Primary icon/accent | `#CC4E0E` |
| Bone White | Backgrounds / text | `#F5F2EB` |
| Charcoal Black | Base/background/weight | `#1D1D1D` |
| Steel Blue | Accent highlights/CTAs | `#3A5A8F` |
| Graphite Gray | Secondary text / UI elements | `#585858` |

* *Use Steel Blue for **CTAs and hyperlinks only.***  
* *Use Burnt Orange **sparingly**—headlines, labels, or system-level icons.*  
* *Bone White should remain your **default canvas color***.

  ---

  ## **Logo Usage Guidelines**

  ### **Approved Use**

* Full logo (Anvil \+ Flame \+ Text) on white, bone, or charcoal backgrounds

* Maintain minimum clear space (1.5x logo height)

* Use monochrome version when color is restricted

  ### **Avoid**

* Resizing individual elements

* Using gradients on flame

* Overlapping on busy/photographic backgrounds

* Changing flame shape

  ---

  ## **Voice & Messaging Rules**

  **Brand Voice:**

* Direct, confident, system-minded

* No fluff, no filler

* Speak like an operator, not a marketer

  ### **Example Tone**

*“We build systems that deliver qualified leads. On repeat.”*  
*“We help visionary businesses tell their story through innovative solutions.”*

---

## **UX \+ Interface Standards**

### **Buttons**

* **Font**: Oswald

* **Fill Color**: Steel Blue (`#3A5A8F`)

* **Text**: White

* **Corner Radius**: 4px

* **Hover**: Darker steel blue tone

  ### **Input Fields**

* **Border**: Graphite Gray

* **Fill**: Bone White

* **Font**: Source Sans / Inter Regular

  ---

  ## **Brand Rules**

  **DO NOT:**

* Use unapproved fonts

* Stack orange \+ blue text in body

* Overuse orange as fill color

* Modify flame icon
